# RIFTWEAVER

## Codex Master Design Specification v1.0

---

# PROJECT SUMMARY

RIFTWEAVER is a single-player procedural survival RPG built around timeline traversal, civilization evolution, historical consequence, and long-term world simulation.

The player is a Riftwalker: a rare being capable of surviving reality collapse and moving between timelines.

The core innovation is that the world remembers.

Player actions influence future civilizations, ecosystems, technologies, religions, myths, and political structures across procedurally generated timelines.

The game combines:

* Survival RPG
* RuneScape-style skilling
* Procedural world generation
* Historical simulation
* Civilization evolution
* Roguelite timeline progression
* Emergent storytelling

---

# DESIGN PILLARS

## Pillar 1 — Persistent Consequence

Every significant player action should have the potential to affect future timelines.

Examples:

* Teaching magic
* Introducing technology
* Starting wars
* Saving bloodlines
* Creating religions
* Destroying ecosystems

Future worlds react accordingly.

---

## Pillar 2 — Discovery Through Time

The player discovers consequences rather than selecting them from menus.

The game reveals historical outcomes through:

* ruins
* descendants
* myths
* books
* architecture
* cultural evolution

---

## Pillar 3 — Living Civilizations

Civilizations evolve independently.

The player influences them but never directly controls them.

Civilizations should feel alive without player intervention.

---

## Pillar 4 — Infinite Exploration

Every timeline generates:

* unique geography
* factions
* history
* ecology
* progression state

No two timelines should be identical.

---

## Pillar 5 — Mystery

The origin of:

* the Grid
* the Rift
* timeline collapse
* Riftwalkers

should never be fully explained.

# WORLD STRUCTURE

## The Grid

A Grid is a self-contained procedural world.

Each Grid contains:

* terrain
* ecosystems
* resources
* civilizations
* monsters
* dungeons
* timeline anomalies

Every Grid eventually collapses.

```text

Biome Seed +
Civilization Seed +
Timeline Age +
Magic Modifier +
Technology Modifier +

Disaster Modifier +
Resource Distribution
```

# TIMELINE SYSTEM

## Timeline Jump

When a Grid collapses:

Player enters a Rift Gate.

Time advances randomly.

Examples:

* 3 weeks
* 12 years
* 87 years
* 600 years
* 2,000 years

The next Grid is generated using prior timeline history.

# SUCCESS METRIC

The player should eventually feel:

"I am not progressing through levels.

I am progressing through history."

The game's defining feature is not combat, crafting, or exploration.

The defining feature is witnessing civilizations evolve from the consequences of your own actions across centuries and millennia.
